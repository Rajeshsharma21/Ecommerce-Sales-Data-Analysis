# Ecommerce-Sales-Data-Analysis


## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive E-commerce Retail Sales Dashboard created using Microsoft Excel.

The dashboard analyzes retail sales data to identify sales trends, top-performing countries, best-selling products, and overall business performance.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze e-commerce sales data and answer questions such as:

- How do sales change over time?
- Which countries generate the highest revenue?
- Which products generate the most revenue?
- Which products sell the highest quantity?
- What is the overall revenue performance?

---

- Microsoft Excel

---

## 📁 Dataset

The dataset contains online retail transaction data including:

- Invoice Number
- Stock Code
- Product Description
- Quantity
- Invoice Date
- Unit Price
- Customer ID
- Country
- Sales Amount

The dataset was cleaned and prepared before analysis.

---

## 📈 Dashboard Features

The dashboard includes:

### 📅 Sales Trend Analysis
Monthly and yearly revenue trends are analyzed using a line chart.

### 🌍 Country-wise Sales Analysis
The top 10 countries are ranked based on revenue generated.

### 🛍️ Top Products by Revenue
The top 10 products generating the highest sales amount are displayed.

### 📦 Top Products by Quantity
The top 10 products sold in the highest quantity are analyzed.

### 💰 Revenue KPI
A Total Revenue KPI card provides a quick overview of overall business performance.

---

## 📊 Key Insights

Some of the key insights identified from the analysis include:

- The United Kingdom is the dominant market in terms of total revenue.
- A small number of countries contribute a significant portion of overall sales.
- Certain products generate significantly higher revenue than other products.
- Product popularity by quantity sold differs from product performance by revenue.
- Sales performance varies across different months and years.
